## Completion scope

The completion target is the strongest honest implementation for legally/publicly accessible or user-provided material. This does **not** mean the system can access every paywalled/private source, every media object, or that reading a source proves its claims true. Missing access remains explicitly missing; metadata/search remains distinct from reading; bounded samples/sections remain distinct from whole-source review; transcript remains distinct from audio/visual analysis; and transformation quality remains distinct from claim truth.
